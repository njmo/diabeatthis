# Product Brief – Intelligent Assistant for Type 1 Diabetes Management

## Introduction / Project Mission
The goal of the application is to create an **intelligent assistant for people living with Type 1 Diabetes (T1D)**, supporting them and their families in daily glucose management.  
The app does not replace therapeutic decisions or insulin pumps, but provides **knowledge, context, and insights** that help users make more accurate and confident choices. Similar to community-driven solutions like AAPS, the app operates **under user responsibility**: it supports but never decides or delivers insulin automatically.  

### Modes of Use
The app offers three flexible modes, tailored to different life stages and contexts:  
1. **Parents of young children (0–7 years)** – parents and close family manage the child’s entire data history, meals, and glucose responses. The app helps anticipate how meals may affect glucose.  
2. **Parent + child (8–18 years)** – joint usage. Parents plan meals (e.g., for school), while the child gets simple instructions on insulin dosing. The app evolves as the child gradually learns independence.  
3. **Adults (18+)** – adults use the app to better plan meals, analyze historical responses, and eliminate guesswork in daily glucose management.  

### Core Assumptions
- **Data integration:** primary data source is **Nightscout** (history of glucose, meals, corrections, insulin).  
- **Manual mode:** the app remains fully intelligent (analyzing data and giving insights), but without automation — all entries must be added manually.  
- **Global reach:** designed from the start as an international solution.  
- **Tone & identity:** supportive, intuitive, yet medically precise.  
- **Regulations:** compliant with GDPR/privacy standards. Not a medical device in the initial stage.  
- **Differentiator:** no other app combines **AI, CGM data, and meal context** in such an intuitive way to create a personalized learning system for each user.  

### Vision
The app eliminates the need to “guess the insulin dose” based on how food looks. Whether in a restaurant, at home, or eating a family recipe, users can immediately see how their body reacted to similar meals in the past. Over time, algorithms become increasingly accurate, offering **personalized and confident support** in daily decisions.

## Business and Product Goals

### Business Goals
- **Mission-driven:** The project was born from the founder’s personal experience as a parent of a child diagnosed with T1D at 13 months. Profit is not the motivation — the primary goal is to **help people live easier, safer lives with T1D**.  
- **Sustainable growth:** the only monetization comes from covering costs of external AI models (e.g., OpenAI, Google).  
- **Partnerships:** collaborations with clinics, foundations, and organizations supporting the T1D community.  
- **Targets:**  
  - Achieve 1,000 active users.  
  - Begin international expansion.  
  - Build recognition within medtech and healthcare communities.  

### Product Goals
- **Simplicity and intuitiveness:** so easy to use that it delivers value from day one without requiring learning curves.  
- **Immediate usefulness:** from the first version, supports daily T1D management using Nightscout data and manual entries.  
- **Mid-term development (12–24 months):**  
  - AI-driven recommendations for meals and activity.  
  - Community and social features (sharing recipes, experiences).  
  - Gamified journaling (inspired by Whoop).  
  - Integrations with Whoop, Garmin, Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava.  
  - Food recognition from photos.  
  - Voice assistant on smartphone and smartwatch.  
  - Intelligent meal suggestions when users “don’t know what to eat.”  
  - Activity-aware dosing support (adjusting insulin to different types of training).

## User Problems and Needs

### Parents of young children  
- **Problem:** impossible to remember how every meal affected glucose.  
- **Need:** a digital diary to analyze history and simplify dosing decisions.  

### Children and teenagers  
- **Problem:** gradual transition to self-management without carrying notebooks of records.  
- **Need:** an evolving tool that grows with their independence.  

### Adults  
- **Problem:** guessing doses in restaurants, managing sport impact, balancing work and self-care.  
- **Need:** quick access to historical insights for meals, recipes, and activities.  

### Sports and activity (shared)  
- **Problem:** exercise strongly influences glucose, but effects vary by type, timing, and intensity.  
- **Need:** the app learns from past training sessions to provide activity-aware dosing support.  

### Shared problem  
- **Human memory is limited** — no one can recall thousands of reactions to food, activity, or corrections.  

### Market gap  
No current solution combines glucose data with context (meals, insulin, activity, trends) to provide:  
- searchable meal/response libraries,  
- contextual recommendations,  
- personalized insights powered by AI.

## Key Success Metrics (KPI)

### Primary Success  
- Helping the founder’s child and other people with T1D manage their condition more effectively.  

### Health KPIs  
- Improved **HbA1c levels** among users.  
- Increased **Time in Range (TIR)** and reduced time in hypo-/hyperglycemia.  
- Fewer errors caused by “guessing” insulin doses.  

### User KPIs  
- Number of users reporting that the app improved their daily management.  
- User retention over time.  
- Engagement with core features (meal logging, AI recommendations).  

### Partnership KPIs  
- Collaborations with institutions such as **NFZ (Poland)** to support doctors and patients.  
- Number of healthcare providers using app-generated reports.  

### Financial KPIs  
- Only measure: covering operational costs related to external AI usage (LLM).  

### Quality of Life KPIs  
- Reduction in stress and mental load.  
- Better sleep (fewer nighttime glucose-related interruptions).  
- Higher confidence and well-being.

## Vision / End Goal

The ultimate vision is to create a **complete intelligent assistant** fully integrated with medical and sports devices.  

### Individual tool and/or community  
- Flexible use: some users prefer **private self-management**, others may be motivated by **sharing with family or community** (recipes, strategies, success stories).  

### Human-centered approach  
- The app will **never replace human decision-making** — its role is to assist, not prescribe. Users remain fully in control.  

### The ultimate change for users  
- **End of guessing insulin doses.**  
- **Peace of mind and confidence** in everyday life with T1D.  
- **Active lifestyle support** without limitations.  
- **Minimal mental time spent on diabetes management** — more time for life.  

### Clinical scope  
- Exclusively focused on **Type 1 Diabetes** to deliver maximum relevance and precision for this group.
