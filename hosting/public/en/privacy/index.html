Controller and contact

The data controller is Marcin Klonica. For privacy questions or to request deletion, email mklonica94@gmail.com. Include the email address you use with the app. Keep Nightscout tokens, passwords and treatment history out of the email.

Sign in with Google

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your account ID, email address, profile name and profile photo, when available. Firebase Authentication uses these details to authenticate your account. Diabeatthis uses them to identify family members, handle invitations and control access. The person approving a pairing request can see the requesting caregiver’s profile details. The app does not require access to your contacts, Gmail or Google Drive files. Google profile data is used to provide features in the app.

Nightscout and family features

The app uses the Nightscout address and optional token entered on the device to retrieve data from the selected instance. Depending on your configuration, the app processes glucose, treatment, meal and activity data. Data stored on the device supports history and use without a continuous connection.

When family features are enabled, Firebase stores data needed for pairing, planning, chat, notifications and sharing selected data with linked family members. Pairing approval determines access. Sending a request does not grant access to history. Notification tokens route alerts to authorised devices.

Optional features

Ingredient search may send a search term or barcode to a catalogue provider. If you use AI features, the app may send the selected provider the information needed for the specific task, such as a label photo or meal or day data for analysis. The provider depends on the feature’s configuration. You can review the result before using it in the app.

Retention and deletion

Chat history is retained according to the family’s settings. The default is seven days. Available settings are one day, seven days, thirty days or no automatic expiry. Account settings and family links are needed while you use the corresponding features. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device. To request deletion of data stored by Diabeatthis on its servers, contact the data controller. Data in your chosen Nightscout instance is subject to that instance’s settings.

Your rights and this website

You may contact the controller about access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection where those rights apply. You may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority. This public website is served by Firebase Hosting. It requires no sign-in and does not display family or health data.
